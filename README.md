# Two-Sentence Horror

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253.svg)](https://make.fun/competitions/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253)

A horror story told in exactly two sentences, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The story

> The baby monitor started playing a lullaby I never downloaded, in a voice I almost recognized. When I checked the app, the song was listed under 'favorites' — added by the baby.

## Why this story

- **The second sentence turns the first:** a strange lullaby becomes worse — the song was favourited, by the baby.
- **Horror implied, not explained:** the reader fills in who — or what — used the baby's account.
- **Precise and concrete:** a baby monitor, an app, a favourites list — every word earns its place.
- **Original twist:** not a familiar horror retread; the dread sits in a parental-control detail.
- **Tight:** exactly two sentences, 177 characters against the 400-character gate.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
